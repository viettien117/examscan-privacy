English Version

ExamScan is a mobile app (iOS and Android) that helps teachers grade multiple-choice answer sheets by scanning the paper with the phone camera or selecting images from the photo library. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what is shared.

1. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or sell any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Account credentials
  • Contact list
  • Precise location (GPS)
  • Microphone audio
  • SMS, call logs
  • Health, fitness, or financial data

Payment details. ExamScan Pro subscriptions are bought and billed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never see or receive your card number, billing address, or store account details.

ExamScan has no user accounts and no login. The app does not send any answer sheet image or grading result to our servers — all recognition and grading happens on your device.

The app does send anonymous usage statistics, crash/performance diagnostics, and a push-notification token to Google Firebase to operate and improve the app and to deliver notifications — see Section 3(c). This never includes your answer sheet images, grading results, or personal identifiers.

3. Data shared with third parties

ExamScan uses four third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

a) Unity LevelPlay / ironSource (advertising)

On Android, the app shows rewarded video advertisements only, through the Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation SDK, and only when you tap the "watch a video for extra credits" button yourself. No ads appear on their own while you work. On iOS, no third-party ad network is currently used at all. The promotional banner at the bottom of the screen is not part of this network on either platform — see section (b).

Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it mediates may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (AAID on Android) for ad measurement and frequency capping
  • Approximate geographic region (country / city) inferred from IP
  • Device model, OS version, screen size, language
  • App bundle id and version
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, video completions, clicks)

The app does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. Unity's full policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. On iOS you can manage ad settings in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising / Tracking; on Android in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Your choice (EEA / UK). If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks you — before the first video — whether ads may be personalized to your interests. If you decline, you still get ads and you still earn exactly the same number of credits; they are simply not selected based on you. You can change your answer at any time in About → Personalized ads. Outside those regions this question is not shown, and ads are not personalized on iOS at all.

We configure our advertising partners to exclude adult and age-inappropriate categories at the strictest setting each partner offers, and we review the ads that appear in the app periodically. Ad networks source their inventory from many advertisers, so we cannot guarantee that every single ad will be suitable. If you see an ad you consider inappropriate, please report it to Rubi.ExamScan@gmail.com and we will block it.

b) GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com)

The app downloads its in-app promotional banner images (these are self-hosted and reviewed by us, entirely separate from the ad network) from a public GitHub repository: https://github.com/viettien117/ExamScan_Ads. GitHub will see your IP address and standard HTTP headers when the app fetches these files. GitHub's policy: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

c) Google Firebase (analytics, crash reporting, remote config, push notifications)

The app uses Google Firebase SDKs to understand usage, improve stability, deliver remote settings, and send notifications. Firebase may collect:

  • Analytics — anonymous app-usage events (screens viewed, features used, including opening the ExamScan Pro screen, the plan chosen and whether a purchase completed) and aggregate device info (model, OS version, language, country)
  • Crashlytics — crash and performance diagnostics (stack traces, device state) used to fix bugs
  • Remote Config — fetches configuration values (e.g., reward amounts) from Google's servers
  • Cloud Messaging — a device push token used to deliver notifications about news, updates, and offers
  • A pseudonymous device / installation identifier to support the above

This data is used only to operate and improve ExamScan and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. You can turn notifications off anytime in your device Settings. Google / Firebase policies: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

d) Google Play Billing / Apple App Store (ExamScan Pro, optional)

ExamScan Pro (unlimited grading, no ads) is an optional subscription sold through Google Play Billing on Android and the App Store on iOS. The store processes the payment, handles renewal and cancellation, and tells the app whether you have an active plan. The app keeps only that status on your device and sends no payment information anywhere. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Google Play or Apple account settings. Policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

5. Children

ExamScan is a tool for teachers and exam markers. Students are the people whose answer sheets get graded, not the intended users of the app. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

We configure every advertising partner to exclude adult and age-inappropriate categories at the strictest setting that partner offers, and we review the ads shown in the app periodically. We cannot guarantee that every ad served by a third-party network will be suitable; report anything inappropriate to Rubi.ExamScan@gmail.com and we will block it.

6. Data retention

  • Images and grading results: kept on your device until you delete them (uninstalling the app removes the cache).
  • Credit counter and language preference: kept on your device; cleared when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • ExamScan Pro status: kept on your device; after reinstalling, the app restores it from your Google Play / App Store account.

7. Your rights

Because the app does not collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on request. You can:

  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device settings to break ad personalization.
  • Email Rubi.ExamScan@gmail.com with any privacy question.

8. Outbound links and sharing

When you tap a link inside the app (for example, "Download templates" in the About screen, or the bug-report email address), the app opens the URL in your default browser or email app. Once you leave ExamScan, your interaction is governed by the privacy policy of the destination site or app — not by us.

The app also includes a "Share app" feature. When you tap it, the system share sheet opens so you can pick an app to forward a short promotional text plus a public link to ExamScan. We do not see who you share with, nor do we collect anything from this action — the only signal ExamScan stores is a local "last share date" used to limit the daily share bonus.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes (e.g., add a new third-party SDK), we will update this page and the "Effective date" above before shipping the change.

Unity LevelPlay và các mạng quảng cáo mà nó trung gian có thể thu thập:

  • Mã định danh quảng cáo (AAID trên Android) — để đo lường và giới hạn tần suất
  • Khu vực địa lý tương đối (quốc gia / thành phố) suy từ IP
  • Kiểu máy, phiên bản hệ điều hành, kích thước màn hình, ngôn ngữ
  • Tên gói + phiên bản app
  • Sự kiện tương tác quảng cáo (hiển thị, xem hết video, click)

App không hiển thị hộp thoại App Tracking Transparency và không theo dõi bạn xuyên app/website của công ty khác. Chính sách Unity: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
